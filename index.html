Before Your Visit

Call the Studio

Arriving about 10 minutes before your scheduled time allows you to check in, share your preferences and settle into the space without feeling rushed.

Speak with your healthcare provider when an injury, recent procedure or medical concern may affect massage. Describe the situation while booking so the studio can discuss whether the appointment should be adjusted or postponed.

Please call as early as possible if your plans change. Current cancellation and rescheduling terms can be confirmed during online booking or by calling the studio.
